Classic Roasted Turkey

🍽️
Serves
12-14
🍳
Method
Roasted
⏱️
Time
4hrs
👨‍🍳
Difficulty
MEDIUM
🔥
Calories
320kcal

Description

This Classic Roasted Turkey is the perfect centerpiece for your holiday table. Featuring golden, crispy skin and tender, juicy meat infused with aromatic herbs and butter, this recipe delivers restaurant-quality results at home.

The secret lies in proper preparation, a flavorful herb butter, and careful roasting to ensure every bite is moist and delicious. Whether you're hosting Thanksgiving, Christmas, or any special gathering, this timeless recipe will become your go-to method for creating a memorable feast that brings everyone together.

Tips

Thaw frozen turkey in the refrigerator (24 hours per 4-5 lbs) for food safety.

Bring turkey to room temperature 1 hour before roasting for even cooking.

Use a meat thermometer - breast should reach 165°F, thigh 175°F for perfect doneness.

Tent with foil if skin browns too quickly, removing it during the last 30 minutes.

Let turkey rest 30 minutes before carving to allow juices to redistribute.

Save drippings to make rich, flavorful gravy for serving alongside the turkey.

Nutrition Facts

Per Serving (6 oz): Protein 42g; Fat 12g; Carbohydrates 0g; Fiber 0g; Sodium 380mg; Sugar 0g; Saturated Fat 3.5g; Cholesterol 125mg; Vitamin B6 25%; Niacin 45%; Zinc 18%; Iron 12%

For the Turkey:

  • 1 whole turkey (12-14 lbs), thawed if frozen
  • 1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 2 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

For the Cavity:

  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1 orange, halved
  • 4-5 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 4-5 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 3-4 fresh sage leaves

For Roasting:

  • 2 cups chicken or turkey stock
  • 2 carrots, roughly chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, roughly chopped
  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • Kitchen twine for trussing

Directions:

  1. Remove turkey from refrigerator 1 hour before roasting. Remove giblets and neck from cavity. Pat turkey completely dry inside and out with paper towels. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).
  2. In a small bowl, combine softened butter, chopped rosemary, thyme, sage, minced garlic, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, and paprika. Mix well to create an herb butter.
  3. Carefully separate the skin from the breast meat by sliding your fingers underneath, being careful not to tear the skin. Spread half of the herb butter under the skin directly onto the breast meat.
  4. Stuff the turkey cavity with quartered onion, lemon halves, orange halves, and fresh herb sprigs. Season the cavity with remaining salt and pepper.
  5. Rub the outside of the turkey with olive oil, then spread the remaining herb butter all over the skin. Tie the legs together with kitchen twine and tuck the wing tips under the body.
  6. Place chopped carrots, celery, and onion in the bottom of a large roasting pan. Set a roasting rack on top and place turkey breast-side up on the rack. Pour 1 cup of stock into the bottom of the pan.
  7. Roast turkey for approximately 3-3.5 hours (about 15 minutes per pound), basting every 45 minutes with pan drippings and adding more stock as needed. If skin browns too quickly, tent loosely with foil.
  8. Turkey is done when a meat thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh reads 175°F and the breast reads 165°F. Remove from oven, tent with foil, and let rest for 30 minutes before carving. Reserve pan drippings for gravy.
