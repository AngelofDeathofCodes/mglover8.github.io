Beef Fried Rice

Description

This savory beef fried rice is a complete one-pan meal loaded with tender beef, fluffy rice, crisp vegetables, and scrambled eggs, all tossed together with a delicious soy-based sauce. It's the perfect way to use leftover rice and creates a restaurant-quality dish in your own kitchen. Quick, satisfying, and packed with flavor, this takeout favorite is better homemade.

Tips

Use day-old rice: This is crucial! Freshly cooked rice is too moist and will become mushy. Day-old refrigerated rice is drier and separates better, creating the perfect fried rice texture. If you must use fresh rice, spread it on a baking sheet and refrigerate for 1-2 hours first.

Don't overcrowd the wok: Cook in batches if necessary. Overcrowding lowers the temperature and steams the ingredients instead of frying them.

Slice beef thinly: For tender beef, slice against the grain into thin strips (1/4-inch thick). Partially freezing the beef for 15-20 minutes makes slicing easier.

Rice varieties: Long-grain white rice (like jasmine) works best. Avoid short-grain or sticky rice varieties.

Storage: Store leftover fried rice in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat in a hot skillet with a splash of oil for best results.

Use the right oil: Vegetable oil, canola oil, or peanut oil work best for high-heat cooking. Avoid olive oil as it has a lower smoke point.

Nutrition Facts

Pre Serving: Protein 32g; Fat 16g; Carbohydrates 48g; Cholesterol 185mg; Sodium 1100mg; Fiber 3g; Sugar 4g

Ingredients

    For the Beef:

  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

    • For Fried Rice:

  • 4 cups cooked white rice (preferably day-old and cold)
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil (divided)
  • 3 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots (or fresh diced carrots and peas)
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 3 green onions, sliced (white and green parts separated)

    • For the Sauce:

  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp seasome oil
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper(or black pepper)

    • For Garnish:

  • Sliced green onions
  • Seasame seeds (optional)

Directions:

  1. Marinate the beef: In a medium bowl, combine sliced beef with 2 tablespoons soy sauce, cornstarch, and 1 teaspoon sesame oil. Mix well and let marinate while you prepare other ingredients (at least 10 minutes)
  2. Prepare the sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, sugar, and white pepper. Set aside.
  3. Prep ingredients: Make sure your rice is cold and broken up (no clumps). Dice vegetables, mince garlic and ginger, slice green onions, and beat the eggs. Having everything ready is key to quick cooking.
  4. In a separate small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, sugar, and white pepper until the sugar dissolves and the sauce is well combined. Set this bowl aside near your cooking area so it's ready to add to the pan.
  5. Cook the eggs: Heat a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil. Pour in the beaten eggs and scramble until just cooked through, breaking them into small pieces. Remove eggs from the wok and set aside.
  6. Cook the beef: Add 1 tablespoon of oil to the same wok over high heat. Add the marinated beef in a single layer and let it sear without moving for 1-2 minutes. Stir-fry for another 2-3 minutes until browned and cooked through. Remove beef from the wok and set aside with the eggs.
  7. Cook the vegetables: Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil to the wok. Add diced onion and cook for 1-2 minutes. Add garlic, ginger, and the white parts of the green onions. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add peas and carrots and cook for 2 minutes. (3-4 minutes)
  8. Add the rice: Add the cold rice to the wok, breaking up any clumps with your spatula. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes, tossing constantly to coat the rice with oil and heat it through. Press the rice against the hot wok to get some crispy bits.
  9. Combine everything: Return the cooked beef and scrambled eggs to the wok. Pour the sauce over everything and toss well to combine. Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes until everything is heated through and well coated with sauce. Add the green parts of the green onions and toss.
  10. Serve: Remove from heat and serve immediately, garnished with additional sliced green onions and sesame seeds if desired.
