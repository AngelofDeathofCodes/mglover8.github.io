BBQ Baby Back Ribs

🍽️
Serves
4-6
🍳
Method
Grilled/Baked
⏱️
Time
3hrs 30'
👨‍🍳
Difficulty
MEDIUM
🔥
Calories
520kcal

Description

These BBQ Baby Back Ribs are the ultimate crowd-pleaser, featuring tender, fall-off-the-bone meat glazed with a rich, smoky barbecue sauce. The secret lies in the low-and-slow cooking method that breaks down the connective tissue while keeping the meat incredibly juicy.

Coated with a flavorful dry rub and slow-cooked to perfection, these ribs develop a beautiful caramelized crust while remaining succulent inside. Whether you're grilling outdoors or baking in the oven, this recipe delivers restaurant-quality ribs that will have everyone asking for seconds. Perfect for summer cookouts, game day parties, or any time you're craving authentic BBQ flavor.

Tips

Remove the membrane from the back of the ribs before seasoning for more tender meat and better flavor penetration.

Let the dry rub sit on the ribs for at least 2 hours or overnight in the refrigerator for maximum flavor.

Cook low and slow at 275°F for the most tender results - rushing the process will result in tough, chewy ribs.

Wrap ribs in foil during the middle of cooking to trap moisture and speed up the tenderizing process.

Apply BBQ sauce only during the last 30 minutes of cooking to prevent burning and achieve a perfect glaze.

Let ribs rest for 10 minutes after cooking before cutting to allow juices to redistribute throughout the meat.

Nutrition Facts

Per Serving (4-5 ribs): Protein 42g; Fat 32g; Carbohydrates 18g; Fiber 1g; Sodium 980mg; Sugar 14g; Saturated Fat 11g; Cholesterol 135mg; Vitamin A 8%; Vitamin C 6%; Calcium 6%; Iron 15%

For the Ribs:

  • 2 racks baby back ribs (about 4-5 lbs total)
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard (as binder)

For the Dry Rub:

  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp cumin

For the BBQ Sauce:

  • 1 1/2 cups ketchup
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)

Directions:

  1. Prepare the ribs: Place ribs bone-side up on a cutting board. Slide a butter knife under the membrane at one end to loosen it, then grab it with a paper towel and peel it off completely. This step is crucial for tender ribs.
  2. Make the dry rub: In a small bowl, combine brown sugar, paprika, black pepper, salt, chili powder, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne pepper, oregano, and cumin. Mix thoroughly until well combined.
  3. Season the ribs: Pat ribs dry with paper towels. Brush both sides lightly with yellow mustard (this helps the rub stick). Generously coat both sides of the ribs with the dry rub, pressing it into the meat. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight for best results.
  4. Make the BBQ sauce: In a medium saucepan, combine ketchup, apple cider vinegar, brown sugar, honey, Worcestershire sauce, molasses, Dijon mustard, smoked paprika, garlic powder, black pepper, and cayenne if using. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring frequently. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 15-20 minutes until thickened. Set aside to cool.
  5. Preheat oven to 275°F (135°C). Line a large baking sheet with aluminum foil. Place ribs meat-side up on the baking sheet. Cover tightly with another layer of foil to create a sealed packet.
  6. Bake covered ribs for 2.5 hours. The meat should be very tender and starting to pull away from the bones. Remove from oven and carefully open the foil packet (watch for hot steam).
  7. Increase oven temperature to 400°F (200°C). Brush ribs generously with BBQ sauce on both sides. Return to oven uncovered and bake for 15 minutes. Remove, brush with more sauce, and bake another 10-15 minutes until sauce is caramelized and sticky.
  8. For grilling method: After step 6, transfer ribs to a preheated grill over medium heat. Brush with BBQ sauce and grill for 5-7 minutes per side, basting with more sauce, until charred and caramelized. Let rest for 10 minutes before cutting between the bones into individual ribs. Serve with extra BBQ sauce on the side and your favorite sides like coleslaw, cornbread, or baked beans.
