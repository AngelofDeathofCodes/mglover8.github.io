Cooking Measurement Conversions

Liquid Volume Conversion Chart (US Customary)

This chart is based on fluid ounces and volume measurements.

Measurement Equivalents
1 Tablespoon (Tbsp) 3 teaspoons (tsp) or 1/2 fluid ounce
1/4 cup 4 Tbsp or 2 fluid ounces
1/2 cup 8 Tbsp or 4 fluid ounces
1 cup 16 Tbsp, 8 fluid ounces, or 1/2 pint
2 cups 1 pint or 16 fluid ounces
4 cups 1 quart or 32 fluid ounces
1 gallon 4 quarts or 128 fluid ounces

Dry Measurement Conversion Chart

Dry measures focus on ensuring proper volume by leveling ingredients. The weight of a "cup" of dry ingredients (like flour vs. sugar) will vary, but their volume equivalents in the US system are consistent.

Measurement Equivalents
1 Tablespoon (Tbsp) 3 teaspoons (tsp)
1/8 cup 2 Tbsp
1/4 cup 4 Tbsp
1/3 cup 5 Tbsp + 1 tsp
1/2 cup 8 Tbsp
2/3 cup 10 Tbsp + 2 tsp
3/4 cup 12 Tbsp
1 cup 16 Tbsp
