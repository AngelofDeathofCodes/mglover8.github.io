Classic Lasagna

🍽️
Serves
8-10
🍳

MEDIUM

450-500kcal

Description

This hearty, comforting lasagna features layers of rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta cheese mixture, tender pasta, and melted mozzarella cheese. It's the ultimate Italian-American comfort food that's perfect for feeding a crowd or meal prepping for the week. Each slice reveals beautiful layers of flavor that come together in perfect harmony.

Tips

Freeze for later: Lasagna freezes beautifully for up to 3 months. Assemble in a disposable aluminum pan, wrap tightly in plastic wrap and foil. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before baking, or bake from frozen at 375°F for 90 minutes covered, then 30 minutes uncovered.

No-boil noodles: You can use no-boil lasagna noodles to save time. Add an extra 1/2 cup of water to your sauce to compensate for absorption, and increase covered baking time by 10 minutes.

Prevent watery lasagna: Make sure your meat sauce is thick (not runny) before assembling. Let the sauce simmer long enough to reduce excess liquid.

Use a roasting pan: If your sauce is very generous, use a deeper roasting pan or place your baking dish on a baking sheet to catch any overflow.

Let it rest: The 15-minute rest time is crucial! Cutting too early will result in a messy, runny lasagna. Patience pays off with clean, beautiful slices.

Leftovers: Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Reheat individual portions in the microwave or reheat covered in a 350°F oven for 20-25 minutes.

Nutrition Facts

Pre Serving: Protein 32g; Fat 22g; Carbohydrates 38g; Cholesterol 95mg; Sodium 950mg; Fiber 4g; Sugar 8g

Ingredients

    For the Meat Sauce:

  • 1 lb ground beef or turkey
  • 1/2 lb Italian sausage (mild or hot)
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
  • 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

    • For the Cheese Mixture:

  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cups fresh parsley,chopped (or 2 tbsp dried)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg (optional)

    • For Assembly:

  • 12-15 lasagna noodle(1 box)
  • 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese(divided)
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese(divided)
  • Fresh basil for garnish(optional)

Directions:

  1. Make the meat sauce: Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and Italian sausage, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Cook for 8-10 minutes until browned. Add diced onion and cook for 5 minutes until softened. Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Prepare the sauce in the pot: Add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, tomato sauce, and water to the meat mixture. Stir well to combine. Add sugar, basil, oregano, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if using. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 30-45 minutes, stirring occasionally. The sauce should thicken as it cooks. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
  3. Cook the noodles: While the sauce simmers, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions until al dente (usually 8-10 minutes). Drain and lay flat on a baking sheet drizzled with olive oil to prevent sticking.
  4. Make the cheese mixture: In a separate medium bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, chopped parsley, salt, pepper, and nutmeg if using. Mix well until smooth and set aside.
  5. TPreheat the oven: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  6. Assemble the lasagna - First layer: Spread 1 1/2 cups of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Arrange 4-5 lasagna noodles over the sauce, overlapping slightly if needed. Spread half of the ricotta mixture over the noodles. Sprinkle with 1 cup of mozzarella cheese and 1/4 cup of Parmesan cheese. Top with 1 1/2 cups of meat sauce. (10 minutes)
  7. Assemble the lasagna - Second layer: Add another layer of 4-5 noodles. Spread the remaining ricotta mixture over the noodles. Sprinkle with 1 cup of mozzarella and 1/4 cup of Parmesan. Top with 1 1/2 cups of meat sauce.
  8. Assemble the lasagna - Final layer: Add the final layer of noodles. Spread the remaining meat sauce over the top. Sprinkle with the remaining 2 cups of mozzarella and 1/2 cup of Parmesan cheese, covering the entire surface.
  9. Bake covered: Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil (spray the underside with cooking spray to prevent cheese from sticking). Bake for 25 minutes.
  10. Bake uncovered: Remove the foil and bake for an additional 25-30 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown on top
  11. Rest before serving: Remove from the oven and let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before cutting. This allows the layers to set and makes slicing much easier. Garnish with fresh basil if desired.
